## Generation prompt

Create a polished premium brand identity exploration board for BAVUSO MOTORS, an original advanced electric long-distance motorcycle company. This is a GRAPHIC DESIGN wordmark exploration, not a motorcycle design render. Five clearly distinct options numbered 1 through 5, organized as FIVE horizontal rows on one tall high-resolution white board. Every row has: small number and short direction label at far left, a large accurately spelled "BAVUSO" custom wordmark in black in the center-left, tiny secondary "MOTORS" underneath where appropriate, and at right a close-up photoreal material application of that SAME row's wordmark as a SMALL subtle satin-metal or engraved badge on a gently curved satin graphite motorcycle panel. Equal row sizes and wordmark cap height for fair comparison. Clear whitespace, refined visual hierarchy, restrained black/graphite/titanium palette, no color accents, no existing brand logos. No full motorcycles, no slogans or performance claims.
Strategic intent: technical precision, advanced engineering, intelligent integration and credible value through technology, understated luxury. Elegant readable typography, recognizable custom details, beautifully executed and restrained; not cyberpunk, not racing graphics, not imitation Tesla lettering. Five genuinely different type structures, not minor spacing variations.
1 label "PRECISION": medium-weight uppercase geometric sans, generous but controlled tracking, intact clear letterforms, a single distinctive subtly squared U and carefully balanced V. Timeless engineered confidence. Badge very small raised brushed titanium letters on graphite.
2 label "INSTRUMENT": narrower technical uppercase sans inspired by high-quality instrument engraving, slightly softened corners, compact economical width, consistent stroke. Subtle recessed silver-filled engraving on graphite, no outline frame.
3 label "MONOLITH": wider low-profile solid uppercase lettering, stout but elegant, broad stance and exact optical spacing, minimal custom chamfer accents without cutting away readability. Flush dark brushed-metal plaque with tonal debossed letters.
4 label "CALIBER": refined humanist technical sans with disciplined proportions and subtle warmth; regular width, precise curved bowls, distinctive open B counterforms yet readable, modest tracking. Micro-engraved unpainted satin titanium insert aligned with a real panel seam.
5 label "SIGNAL": lightweight uppercase architectural sans, open spacing, long calm horizontal rhythm, hairline-to-light strokes still manufacturable, a subtle original BV monogram shown small BELOW the main wordmark as an optional secondary symbol, no shields/wings/lightning bolts. Tonal dark nickel applied lettering on smooth silver body panel.
Exact primary text in every row BAVUSO (B A V U S O), optional secondary MOTORS, labels as given. Technical luxury should come from proportion and execution, not ornament. Render all five wordmarks flat straight-on; badge detail can be oblique. Keep badge application much smaller than main wordmark. No mock handwritten signature, no serif, no italic speed slashes, no fake technology claims.
