Owner feedback driving this round: long trips are a major use case; comfort/performance/style with less racer character. Reference 3 has appealing compact, controllable but substantial proportions; simplify its visuals. References 15–16 lead aesthetically but need travel comfort. Reference 18's simple, comfortable yet capable appearance is appealing, interpreted more upright and adventurous. Avoid bulky or front-heavy touring shapes, thin/upturned projecting seats, dirt-bike seats, busy layers and Ninja-like styling. Optional saddlebags should be possible as extras, with bags omitted from these views.

Review limits: concepts converged more closely than intended. Rendered drive details vary from the intended belt reference, and several tails retain a raised passenger section. Do not interpret depicted internals, seating comfort, exact scale, lighting compliance, mass, cooling or aerodynamic performance as validated. First select visual features, then reconcile a measured rider/component layout.
