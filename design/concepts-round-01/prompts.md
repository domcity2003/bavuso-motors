# Bavuso concept round 01

User direction: long-trip comfort, performance and style; compact, visually simple, clean electric aesthetic; supportive low seat and raised handlebars; optional bags omitted. Same scale, blank background.

Built-in image_gen tool. These are styling explorations, not verified packages.

## 1. Balanced Road Tourer

Use case: product-mockup. Generate ONE photorealistic industrial design studio render of an original Bavuso electric long-distance road motorcycle, not a collage. Blank pure white seamless background, subtle contact shadow only. Exact true left SIDE PROFILE, front wheel pointing LEFT, no perspective foreshortening, camera at axle height, long lens orthographic-like catalog elevation. Landscape 3:2 composition. Whole motorcycle centered, both tires on same horizontal baseline at 79% image height; motorcycle occupies 84% image width. Maintain identical scale across concept series: 1425 mm wheelbase, 17-inch alloy wheels, front tire 120/70 and rear 160/60, seat around 790mm. No people or scenery. Same satin light silver bodywork, graphite black lower panels, black supportive seat, dark metal wheels for honest form comparison. Small neutral numeral in top left only as specified. Original design must blend compact Moto Guzzi V100 Mandello-like controllable proportions with clean contemporary electric aesthetic associated with Honda WN7 and Verge, plus comfort and integrated body simplicity appreciated in Honda NM4, interpreted more upright and road-adventurous. No brand copying, no existing logos. Long trip comfort, performance and style; not a racer. Wide supportive substantially padded rider seat with a relatively level usable surface, rounded edges and narrow seat nose. Seat clearly BELOW handlebars; visibly raised comfortable bars with modest reach, mid-mounted foot pegs BELOW rider hips, open knee angle, no rearsets. Integrated supporting tail, no thin kicked-up floating seat or extreme tail overhang. Compact visual mass evenly distributed; no huge front-heavy fairing, no bulky luxury tourer. No saddle bags, no top box, no ugly luggage racks; subtle capped optional bag mounting provisions only. Real mechanical package: protected central battery region approximately 600mm long x260mm wide x560mm high between wheels beneath upper body, no combustion engine, no exhaust, no hubless wheel. Frame-mounted compact motor near swingarm pivot, conventional telescopic fork, double-sided swingarm, belt final drive with guard, realistic brake discs/calipers, sensible tire clearances and 130mm wheel travel. Meaningful wind protection and credible cooling inlet AND exit, small practical mirrors on both sides, road lighting, turn signals, license plate bracket. Refined simple body panels with plausible manufacturing seams, accessible fasteners, no fake vents, ornamental winglets, sci-fi glowing strips, exposed orange cables or excessive surface layering. Side shape and ergonomics must be realistic though this is an unvalidated design concept. 
Concept 1: Balanced Road Tourer. The balanced all-rounder: calm flowing upper line, gently rounded wedge-shaped compact nose, moderate clear windscreen smoothly integrated above headlight, fairly complete clean fairing protecting torso and knees, compact robust tail and lightly stepped thick passenger pad integrated into tail. Mid-height bar slightly rear of screen, relaxed forward lean. Single broad flowing silver side panel with one carefully defined crease and small functional dark cooling outlet. Closest to elegant compact road-tourer, no beak.
