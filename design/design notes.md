## Product and shape

Bavuso's motorcycle is its only product and must carry its brand identity. Long trips are a major use case: balance comfort, performance and style with less racer character. The selected starting point is draft A from round 02, developed from original concept 2, informed by the owner's interest in concepts 4 and 5.

Preserve compact, controllable proportions with a sturdy highway presence. Keep visual mass balanced; avoid bulky, front-heavy touring bodywork. Use a small number of clean, purposeful surfaces, an integrated central battery enclosure and a supportive tail. Avoid busy layering, decorative vents, aggressive racing graphics and thin projecting sport-bike seats.

## Rider comfort intent

Rider should sit comfortably near upright, with shoulders relaxed, elbows softly bent and wrists neutral. Hands should rest naturally on the grips; the rider should not need to prop their torso on the handlebars during ordinary steady riding. The latest baseline uses higher, swept-back bars with reduced reach, a flatter padded saddle and mid-position pegs. This is design intent, not verified ergonomics or a promise of zero hand load under braking, acceleration or road impacts.

Engineering should establish the seat/bar/peg relationship in an adjustable seating buck with riders of varied proportions and protective gear. Check ground reach, seat width/support and slope, knee/hip comfort, wrist and lever alignment, full steering lock, visibility and cable/hose movement. The rider illustration in final-review-01 is only an illustrative posture hypothesis and must not be used as a dimensional template.

## Color and materials

One principal matte body color per option: black, silver or tan. Keep seat, tires, wheels, controls, battery protection and other functional components in a consistent dark palette. Black is a required option. Silver retains the technical metallic character the owner liked, with subdued matte reflection. Tan provides a warmer alternative. Validate paint durability, cleaning, UV/weathering, abrasion and repair with physical samples; generated shading is not a coating specification.

## Branding

Understated technical precision and luxury. Use a small BAVUSO uppercase wordmark on the upper side panel, with restrained titanium/tonal contrast. The brand should be recognized through the motorcycle's form and execution. Avoid oversized plaques, repeated logos, large battery graphics, racing typography and ornamental emblems. Exact letterforms and manufacturing method remain open; Motors may be used in formal company materials rather than on the visible bike badge.

Technology should supply a credible reason for value through demonstrated efficiency, integration, usability and quality. Do not present unvalidated range, weight, charging or network access as delivered features.

## Luggage and everyday use

Allow optional saddlebags as an extra; the base bike is shown without them. Engineer discreet attachment points and proper subframe load paths without a permanently bulky rack. Bag capacity, passenger rating and payload remain undecided. Consider wet/gloved controls, clear charge/ready/limited-power feedback, practical charging access, stand operation, parking and repair access.

## Engineering boundaries

Use [engineering handoff](../engineering-handoff.md) and [designer brief](../designer-brief.md) as the mechanical baseline. The renders do not establish a buildable package.

- Preserve the approximately 600 mm fore/aft × 260 mm wide × 560 mm high battery study region until real module, cooling, protection and service packaging supports changes. It is not necessarily a rectangular final pack.
- Verify installation space for the frame-mounted motor near the swingarm pivot, inverter, charger, charging electronics, real radiators/fans/pumps and cable/hose bends. Shown cooling openings are visual intent, not a thermal design.
- Retain the conventional fork, double-sided swingarm and belt-drive study; reconcile AI-rendered brake/drive/suspension details with selected hardware. Check full bump, steering, tire growth, lean and service clearances.
- Upright posture and raised bars must be evaluated together with the whole bike/rider aero target of CdA ≤0.33 m², highway buffeting and 77 mph crosswind behavior. No measured drag or stability result exists for this shape.
- Resolve inlet placement, parked cable reach, drainage, interlocks and hot-weather stationary charging. Native NACS does not establish Tesla network authorization; 120 V support is not established by the candidate charger.
- Reconcile the complete mass budget. The current reference is approximately 433 lb before allowance / 446 lb with it. Under 400 lb remains preferred and unproven; the body/seat/controls/lights/wiring allocation is shared, not a fairing-only allowance.
- Current EMRAX v1.7 supplier revision changes are flagged in [design research](../research/design-strategy.md). Confirm thermal ratings, maps and supply before fixing the motor package.
- Road-equipment placement, manufacture, structure, fatigue, braking and applicable certification work remain engineering tasks.
