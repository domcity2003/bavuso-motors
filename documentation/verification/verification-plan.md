# Vehicle verification plan — development issue 0.1

October 25, 2026, US Central. Prepared by Codex (GPT-6 family).

**No physical verification in this plan has been completed.** The existing PowerShell models are feasibility evidence only. This document defines the evidence an engineering team must produce and the sequence needed to avoid building an expensive collection of incompatible parts. It is a development plan, not permission to energize a pack or ride a prototype.

## Test configuration and evidence contract

Every result must name its test ID, requirement, approved procedure/revision, vehicle or specimen serial, part revisions, cell lot, software/calibration hashes, selected accessories, loading and measured axle weights, SOC/health, ambient conditions, instruments/calibration, operator, raw data, analysis version, result, anomalies and independent reviewer. Preserve failures and aborted runs. A subsequent pass does not erase a failure's cause or required corrective action.

Use the terms **planned**, **procedure approved**, **executed**, **passed**, **failed**, **invalid** and **blocked** distinctly. An incomplete procedure or missing acceptance threshold is blocked, not passed. A test run at an unapproved configuration is development information until its relevance is reviewed. No requirements are closed by render, supplier headline or an uncorrelated simulation.

The owner controls product trades. Responsible engineering leads approve numerical engineering limits, and the compliance lead approves the applicable legal test matrix. Reviewer assignments are roles to staff, not fictional signatures. Production release requires a named person accepting each role.

Gate approval is scoped. A component bench release cannot be read as whole-vehicle road permission. A styling update, pack chemistry change, brake change or inverter firmware change reopens affected gates through an engineering change assessment.

## Requirements awaiting numerical closure

Record these as owner decisions or engineering proposals before freezing the relevant procedures: solo versus passenger rating; rider/cargo envelope and GVWR/GAWR; luggage capacity; claimed range temperature, wind, SOC endpoint and battery age; minimum top-speed duration and allowable SOC; ambient operating/storage/charging envelope; battery service life and warranty; full-power duration; acceptable hot-weather charging time; weather sealing and immersion claim; ride quality and buffeting limits; ground clearance and lean angle; service intervals; production volume/cost. Do not treat the 90 kg model rider as payload rating or a 200-mile new-battery calculation as an all-weather life guarantee.

## Range evidence quality

At 70 mph, 200 miles takes approximately 2 h 51 min before stops. Define whether the claim is continuous steady-speed energy equivalence or measured route range; present them separately. Opposing-direction runs help characterize wind/gradient but cannot cancel nonlinear wind drag by averaging speed alone. Record air speed/yaw, elevation, ambient density and road surface. Use independent calibrated pack current/voltage integration and SOC logging; do not infer used energy only from a dashboard percentage. Test speed management must not encourage unsafe public-road use; use a suitable controlled facility.

Disclose accessory state, heated clothing, ambient temperature, tire pressure, rider size, screen setting and battery condition in any reported result. Repeat tests enough to estimate uncertainty; a proposed minimum of three valid representative runs is an initial development suggestion, not a reliability demonstration. Statistical production claim confidence needs a separate sample plan. A favorable single small-rider run does not establish the normal-rider claim.

## Safety and fault verification logic

The [hazard register](hazard-analysis.csv) is a preliminary hazard analysis, not an ISO 26262 functional-safety certification or completed FMEA. Each hazard needs severity/exposure/controllability assessment with actual intended use, a fault-tree/system architecture analysis, detection coverage, time-to-harm, fault-tolerant time interval, common-cause review and residual-risk acceptance. Do not assign a numerical RPN to unknown probabilities merely to make the table look complete.

Begin fault injection on models/HIL and isolated fixtures; progress to energy-bearing hardware only after qualified test engineering approves facility, containment, instrumentation and emergency arrangements. A fault response that abruptly removes useful deceleration or propulsion in traffic can itself be hazardous. The controls team must analyze graduated limitation versus immediate interruption by fault type rather than treating every fault as the same contactor-opening command.

No road-test rider serves as an electrical protection experiment. No abuse test is performed on the only prototype intended for later riding without a formal inspection/disposition. Any crash, overtemperature, isolation fault, swelling, unexpected torque or brake anomaly stops expansion of the relevant test envelope pending investigation.

## Review and reporting

Maintain one verification cross-reference: requirement → design item → hazard/control → procedure → result → configuration → approver. Every open test above has a named owner before G2; staffing is presently unassigned. A weekly internal review can be adopted by the team, but no automation is created by this document. At each gate report passed, failed, blocked and untested items, test deviations, mass growth and unresolved owner trades. A planned test is never counted as evidence that the bike meets a goal.

For the regulatory starting points and applicability limitations, use [compliance source record](../research/compliance-sources.md). The authoritative test procedure must include the complete applicable legal text and actual vehicle category rather than relying on this short matrix.
