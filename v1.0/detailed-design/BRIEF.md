## The product
Bavuso V1.0 is an electric adventure crossover motorcycle. It should be premium, realistic and buildable, with a unit cost of about $30–35k or less at scale. The CAD and studies so far are a **packaging and concept model**. This package turns it into a **detailed design**: each discipline gets real analysis, sized parts, margins and a verdict.

## Rules
- **Explain simply and lead with the plain answer.** The reader is Dominic, a finance person who builds with AI (not an engineer by training), so plain language with numbers.
- **Verify before calling a result good.** Run a sanity check on every analysis:
  - a hand calculation against the FEA;
  - a mesh-convergence check;
  - energy balance for thermal;
  - force balance for CFD.
  - Say what was not checked.
- **Survey public prior art first.** Use published data from Zero, Energica, LiveWire and KTM, supplier datasheets, standards and textbooks (Cossalter *Motorcycle Dynamics*, Foale). Cite URLs.
- **Never read or copy secrets** (.env, tokens, keys).
- **Report format:**
  - Write your report to `detailed-design/<your-file>.md`.
  - Stamp the header "November 12, 2026, US Central. Prepared by Claude Opus 5.5 (claude-opus-5-5)."
  - Start with "## The plain answer".
  - Give a **verdict table**: item, value, required, margin, pass/fail/open.
  - Add **"CAD change requests"** with exact numbers.
  - End with **"What this does not prove"**.
- **Files:** scripts go in `detailed-design/tools/<discipline>-*.py`. Raw outputs go in `detailed-design/<discipline>/`. Keep big solver output (meshes, time dirs) out of git: put it under `detailed-design/<discipline>/run/`, which is ignored.
- **Final reply to the orchestrator** (under 300 words): the verdicts, the CAD change requests, and any blocking problem.
