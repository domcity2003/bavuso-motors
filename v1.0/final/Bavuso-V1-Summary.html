Bavuso V1: Electric Adventure-Touring Motorcycle

Rev E design freeze · November 22, 2026
V1 is a deliberate compromise. The original goals, under 400 lb and 200 miles at 70 mph, cannot both be met with today’s cells: 400 lb carries only about 11 kWh (about 74 mi) and 200 mi needs about 32 kWh and a 631 lb bike. V1 is the buildable optimum: about 258 kg / 569 lb, 23.8 kWh, about 150 mi at 70 mph, 125 mph sustained, native NACS charging, with a planned path to better range and lower mass.

Bavuso V1  Summary

How it was designed, and what it proves

Design

  • Pack: Molicel P60B 21700, 100s11p, in a structural aluminium case that forms the lower body (108.0 kg).
  • Drive: EMRAX 228 and Cascadia CM200DX, 8 mm Gates belt at 3.60:1 (23.65 kg).
  • Chassis: steel frame with cast nodes, steel pivot yoke, cast A356 single-sided arm (about 31 kg).
  • Speed-scheduled semi-active steering damper; carbon wheels; 320 mm front discs.

Analysis done

  • Structures (FEA), thermal, dynamics, drive and electrical models; mass ledger tied to the BOM; independent review.
  • Cost $29.5k at 10k/yr, $39.3k at 1k, $58.0k at 100 (estimates).
  • 91 of 91 CAD envelope checks; 45-row compliance matrix; 48-row DFMEA; $7.04M, 28-month verification plan.

Mass: 328 to 258 kg

  • Concept ledger 328.4 kg, reconciled 319.6 kg, rev C CAD 255.3 kg, final 257.9 kg.
  • The pack, body panels and wiring gave most of the saving; the chassis grew to about 31 kg because the belt-on-one-side layout needs a steel yoke.
  • Mass headroom is 2.1 kg; the ledger’s growth rule gives about 287 kg.

Limits

  • Range is calculated at an assumed CdA (break-even about 0.407).
  • Real-world range about 15-20% lower than calculated.
  • No part weighed, no test run, no supplier quote.
  • Supercharger access not established.

Roadmap: V1.1 and beyond

Better cells (more range at the same mass), lower drag (aero refinement), a lighter chassis (about 10 kg of identified levers: wheels and brakes, short HV bus, aluminium yoke, cell-to-chassis pack, flush radiator pods), styling refinement (pods, edge radii) and a two-up variant.

Status. A simulation- and analysis-based design. Nothing has been built or physically tested; supplier prices are estimates. Aerodynamic validation in progress: range calculated at an assumed CdA of 0.38 m². The full dossier (Bavuso-V1-Design-Dossier.pdf) carries the validation, cost, compliance and verification detail.
